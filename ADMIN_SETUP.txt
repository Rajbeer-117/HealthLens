HEALTHLENS TEAM (ADMIN) SETUP

Patients use HealthLens normally: Summary, Profile, Documents and the chatbot. They never see
anything about administration: no link, no script or style file mentions it, and /admin returns the
same "page not found" as any address that does not exist (also when signed out).

Where it is stored: team access is kept in its own database table (team_admins), separate from the
users table. The role is checked in the database on every request; nothing a patient's browser sends can grant it.
After signing in, team members land on the System dashboard and patients land on their Summary.

Team members (admins) use the same app, upload their own records like any patient, and see one
extra tab: "System dashboard" (statistics on users, sign-ins, uploads, storage and chatbot
response times; no health readings and no chatbot questions are ever shown there).

Giving team (admin) access

A. The FIRST team member (once, on the computer running the app):
   1. Sign up for a normal account at /register.
   2. Stop the app (Ctrl+C) and, in the project folder, run:
          python app.py --make-admin USERNAME
      Type that account's password when asked (it is not displayed).
   3. Start the app again (python app.py) and sign in. You land on the System dashboard.

B. Everyone after that, from the System dashboard (no terminal needed):
   1. The new team member signs up for a normal account at /register.
   2. A current team member opens System dashboard > Team access. Every account is listed with its
      role. Click "Grant access" on the new member's row, type YOUR OWN password in the pop-up and
      confirm. A message confirms the change ("Team access granted to ...").
   3. The new member's next page load shows the System dashboard tab.

Revoking team access: click "Revoke access" on that member's row and confirm with your own password.
Your own row shows "You" instead of a button: you cannot revoke your own access (another team member
must do it), so the team can never lock itself out. From the terminal:  python app.py --remove-admin USERNAME

Safety: the Team access table and pop-up only exist for signed-in team members (everyone else gets "page not
found", including for the form's address). Every change needs the team member's own password and a
CSRF token, uses parameterised SQL, and is recorded in the activity log (team_grant / team_remove;
wrong passwords are logged as team_access_denied). Patients cannot give themselves access.

Do not deploy this local academic demo publicly or upload real patient information.
