HealthLens: documents uploaded one by one
==========================================

WHY
The course dataset gives each patient one workbook with a full year of data. That is for building and
testing the analysis. In real life a patient never has everything in one place: they get a lab report,
export readings from a blood-pressure monitor or a fitness tracker, keep a visit note. HealthLens therefore
takes documents ONE AT A TIME, reads the values out of each one in the back end, and builds up the patient's
record document by document. Risk levels and risk factors are calculated from whatever has been uploaded,
even a single document, and the page names the measures that are still missing.

WHAT CAN BE UPLOADED (Documents page)
  Lab report, PDF with selectable text   test names in common variants ("LDL-C", "Hemoglobin A1c",
                                          "Glucose, Fasting"...); SI units used in Canada (mmol/L, umol/L,
                                          g/L, nmol/L, pmol/L) converted; reference ranges; collection date;
                                          Patient ID, sex and age (or date of birth)
  Lab report, Word (.docx) or text (.txt) same as PDF
  Clinic visit note (.txt/.docx/.pdf)     blood pressure, heart rate, weight (kg or lb), BMI, SpO2, temperature
  CSV export                              lab results, home blood-pressure monitor, fitness tracker (steps,
                                          sleep in hours or minutes, heart rate, HRV...), period tracker,
                                          symptoms, medications
  Excel workbook (.xlsx/.xlsm)            the HealthLens format, partial workbooks, or any sheet whose
                                          columns look like one of the tables above
  ZIP of workbooks                        many patients at once (used for the 900-patient testing)
  Photo / scan (.png/.jpg)                read only if the optional OCR tool Tesseract is installed
                                          (see below); otherwise stored for reference

Safety: a value is only recorded if its unit fits the test and the value is physically possible, so a
misread number (for example from a blurry photo) never becomes a false flag. Documents without readable
values are stored only, with the reason shown. Nothing is guessed or inferred.

WHOSE RECORD A DOCUMENT JOINS (one account = one patient)
  The record belongs to the account from its first upload; its key never changes and never comes from a
  document. A name, Patient ID, sex or date of birth printed in a document is a FACT about that document,
  compared with what the record already knows. The back end decides; there is nothing for the patient to
  confirm (healthlens/record_rules.py):
    Reject     the document shows a different NAME, SEX or DATE OF BIRTH than the record, an age that does
               not fit the date of birth, or period data while the record is male:
               "note.txt lists name Jane Doe, but 01_lab_report_2025-09-25.pdf says Maya Patel. Not added."
    Reject     the document shows a Patient ID not seen on this record before, unless the SAME NAME or the
               SAME DATE OF BIRTH shows it is the same person (labs and clinics each issue their own IDs).
    Accept     no Patient ID (device exports), a Patient ID already on the record, the first document,
               a new Patient ID with the same name or date of birth, old reports, partly overlapping reports.
    Leave out  single impossible readings (e.g. glucose 9000 mg/dL, a reading dated in the future); the rest of
               the document is kept and the Documents page says how many were left out.
  Names are compared ignoring capitals, punctuation and word order, and a middle name or initial may be
  added: "PATEL, Maya R." matches "Maya Patel"; "Jane Doe" does not match "John Doe".
  Deleting a document removes everything it contributed, including a Patient ID, name, sex or date of birth
  that no remaining document shows, so a wrong first upload cannot lock the record.
  Team (admin) accounts may hold several records, to test the provided 900-patient dataset: there the
  Patient ID still decides the record, as before.
  The same file uploaded twice is not stored again, and the same reading in two documents is counted once.

RISK SCORE, RISK LEVEL AND RISK FACTORS (Summary page) - TRAINED MODEL
  The business partner's dataset is used to TRAIN the model (train_risk_model.py), as asked:
    training data  the 900 patient workbooks (patient_data.zip, a mix of synthetic and real records)
    inputs         measurements only: labs, vitals, wearable data, menstrual cycles, age and sex
                   (medical history and medications are never inputs)
    answer key     the patient's recorded medical history names a related condition
                   (metabolic: prediabetes / type 2 diabetes / borderline glycemic markers;
                    cardiovascular: hypertension / hyperlipidemia; women's health: PCOS)
    method         one logistic-regression model per category; 80% of patients train it, 20% that it never
                   saw test it; whole sources (all labs, all vitals...) are hidden at random during training
                   so it also works from a single document
    result on the 20% test patients (AUC, 0.5 = chance, 1.0 = perfect):
                   metabolic 0.82 (old rules 0.72),
                   cardiovascular 0.92 (old rules 0.59),
                   women's health 1.00 (only 14 patients with PCOS, 3 in the test set: not reliable)
    details        validation/risk_model_report.md; weights in healthlens/risk_model.json
  For each category the patient sees:
    risk score     0-100 from the trained model, using all their documents
    risk level     Low (< 35), Moderate (35-64), High (65+)
    risk factors   the measures that raised the score most
    missing inputs "2 of 6 key inputs missing: A and B. This prediction is based on the other 4 (...), so the
                   prediction score may not be accurate"
  No confidence or uncertainty value is shown (business partner, kickoff). It is an educational score,
  not a diagnosis. If the model file is missing, the rule-based level is used instead.
  Retrain:  python train_risk_model.py   (about 2 minutes)

TRY IT
  sample_documents/ holds 8 synthetic documents for patient PC0303 (2 lab reports in different units,
  a Word lab report, a lab CSV, a home blood-pressure export, a fitness-tracker export, a period
  tracker export, a clinic visit note, and a photo of a lab report). Sign up, then upload them one at a
  time on the Documents page and watch the Summary page build up. They were made from PC0303.xlsx with
  validation/make_sample_documents.py.

OPTIONAL: READING PHOTOS (OCR)
  Windows: install Tesseract from https://github.com/UB-Mannheim/tesseract/wiki, tick "Add to PATH",
  then:  pip install pytesseract pillow
  Mac:     brew install tesseract   then   pip install pytesseract pillow
  Restart the app. Without it, photos are stored only.

LIMITS (next steps)
  Scanned PDFs without selectable text need OCR (stored only for now). Report layouts that put the test
  name and its value on different lines may be missed. Free-text clinical notes are stored, not interpreted.
